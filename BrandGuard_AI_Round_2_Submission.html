02 List of Completed Features (Round 2 Build)

🗂️ 1. Brand Asset & Guideline Vault

Central repository pre-seeded with enterprise brand profiles (Inkloom AI, TechCorp Cloud, Consumer Goods). Ingests and decomposes rules into tone vectors, prohibited terms, approved factual metrics, and design tokens.

🕸️ 2. Policy Knowledge Graph (GraphRAG)

NetworkX-driven directed policy graph linking Rules → Prohibited Terms → Source Documents → Mandatory Disclaimers with exact line-level citation retrieval.

🔍 3. Deterministic Claim Verifier

Detects unsupported superlatives, unverified pricing figures, and performance claims, cross-referencing against active fact sheets with zero hallucination risk.

🎨 4. WCAG 2.1 AA Contrast Engine

Mathematical luminance evaluator (PIL/OpenCV) computing contrast ratios against W3C relative luminance standards (4.5:1 AA / 7.0:1 AAA) and Delta-E color distance.

✍️ 5. Intent-Preserving Diff Studio

Interactive side-by-side diff visualizer with single-click "Accept Fix" buttons that drop compliant phrasing into the draft while preserving author voice.

👥 6. Role-Based Review & Audit Handoff

Collaborative workflow supporting Creator, Brand Manager, and Legal roles with discussion threads, revision status, and SHA-256 audit tokens.

04 Explanation of Changes Made to Original Plan

During the build phase, three pragmatic enhancements were introduced to increase real-world reliability:

  1. Dual-Engine Architecture (Zero-Failure Guarantee): We implemented a full client-side evaluation fallback alongside the FastAPI backend. If the backend is ever unreachable or when running on static deployments, the frontend automatically falls back to an embedded browser-side rule engine, ensuring 100% availability for evaluating judges.
  2. Unified Single-Port Static Hosting: FastAPI was configured to automatically serve the compiled frontend production bundle from /dist, allowing users and judges to run the entire application on a single port (8000) via run_demo.bat with zero setup.
  3. Cryptographic Audit Tokens: Implemented SHA-256 compliance certificate generation for approved content, providing an immutable audit trail for legal compliance verification.

05 The Three Major Improvements for Round 3 (The Finish)

Improvement 1: Autonomous Visual Auto-Correction

Generative image inpainting and automated CSS/SVG palette adjustment that automatically fixes off-palette graphics and low contrast without manual graphic designer re-work.

Improvement 2: Multi-Brand & Regional Localization

Hierarchical policy inheritance (Global Brand → Regional Sub-brand) with automatic detection of regional cultural idioms, translated nomenclatures, and localized FTC/ASA legal disclaimers.

Improvement 3: Synthetic Brand Stress-Testing Agent

Autonomous adversarial agent that generates synthetic edge-case marketing drafts to simulate whether newly written corporate brand guidelines contain loopholes before organization-wide rollout.
