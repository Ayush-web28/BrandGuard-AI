# Inkloom AI — Official Brand Playbook & Governance Standards
Version: 2.4 (2026 Edition)
Author: Brand Strategy & Legal Compliance

## 1. Executive Mission
Inkloom AI provides verifiable brand governance infrastructure for modern high-velocity enterprises. We believe brand integrity is maintained not through fear or heavy friction, but through explainable, intelligent co-piloting.

## 2. Core Voice & Tone Attributes
- **Authoritative:** Speak from evidence, engineering benchmarks, and verifiable proofs.
- **Approachable:** Never talk down to the user; keep language simple and human.
- **Forbidden Buzzwords:** Avoid hollow hype phrases like 'game-changer', 'revolutionary', 'silver bullet', 'paradigm shift', or 'disruptive'.

## 3. Product Nomenclature
The flagship platform is strictly written as **BrandGuard AI**. 
- Never write 'BGAI'
- Never write 'Brandguard' or 'Brand Guard'
- Never write in all lowercase 'brandguard ai'

## 4. Legal, Promotional Claims & Fact Substantiation
1. **Zero Unsubstantiated Superlatives:**
   Never state '100% bug-free', 'zero downtime', 'zero errors', or 'the only AI tool'.
   Approved substitute: Reference specific verified metrics, e.g., 'sub-50ms latency' or '99.9% uptime SLA'.
2. **Mandatory AI Disclosure:**
   In compliance with FTC advertising guidelines and the EU AI Act, any marketing collateral created or assisted by AI must disclose AI usage (e.g., 'Generated with AI assistance').

## 5. Visual Identity & Accessibility
- **Primary Color:** Inkloom Indigo (`#4F46E5`)
- **Secondary Accent:** Cyan (`#06B6D4`)
- **Deep Neutral:** Slate 900 (`#0F172A`)
- **Contrast Ratio:** Text on graphics must strictly pass WCAG 2.1 AA luminance contrast of at least 4.5:1.
