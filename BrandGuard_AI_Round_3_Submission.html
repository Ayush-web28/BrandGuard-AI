02 The Three Promised Major Improvements Delivered

Improvement 1: Autonomous Visual Auto-Correction & Inpainting

What Was Built: An autonomous computer-vision module (PIL/NumPy) that mathematically evaluates W3C relative luminance, detects contrast failures, and solves for the optimal compliant hex token from approved brand palettes via Delta-E minimization.

What It Changes: Automatically recolors off-palette graphics and elevates contrast (e.g. 1.48:1 → 5.53:1 WCAG AA) with 1-click inpainting, eliminating manual graphic designer rework.

Improvement 2: Hierarchical Multi-Brand & Regional Inheritance

What Was Built: A directed policy inheritance engine allowing parent global brands (Inkloom Global HQ) to cascade core standards to regional subsidiaries (North America, European Union, United Kingdom).

What It Changes: Automatically enforces regional statutory consumer protection laws (US FTC 16 CFR Part 255, EU AI Act Reg 2024/1689, UK ASA CAP Code) alongside global brand rules.

Improvement 3: Adversarial Brand Stress-Testing & Loophole Agent

What Was Built: An autonomous adversarial simulation agent in stress_tester.py that generates 6-8 synthetic evasion vectors (semantic synonyms, deceptive zero-dollar claims, obfuscated AI wording).

What It Changes: Computes brand policy resilience (0-100%), exposes rule loopholes, and enables 1-click policy fortification directly into the Brand Vault.

05 Changes from Original Proposal & Known Limitations

Architectural Evolutions Completed Across Rounds:

  1. Dual-Engine Architecture: Implemented a full client-side evaluation fallback alongside FastAPI to ensure evaluators in restricted proxy or offline environments can test all capabilities with zero downtime.
  2. Unified Single-Port Deployment: Replaced separate frontend/backend port architectures with a unified server serving the compiled React bundle on port 8000 via run_demo.bat.
  3. BrandGuard Studio Redesign: Replaced generic AI SaaS styling with a bespoke editorial aesthetic (cream #F5F1E9, ink #202820, terracotta #D97745) featuring an interactive Brand Vault and visual GraphRAG Node Explorer.
  4. Expanded Regulatory Coverage: Broadened beyond basic tone checks to enforce international legal frameworks (EU AI Act Reg 2024/1689, US FTC Endorsement Guides, UK ASA CAP Code).

Transparent Statement of Known Limitations:

  • Real-time WebSocket Presence: Revision history, comments, and approvals persist seamlessly, but live multi-cursor presence across concurrent users currently requires page refresh.
  • Direct Marketplace CMS Plugins: The pre-publish webhook gate (/api/webhook/publish) fully enforces policy via HTTP headers, but native 1-click marketplace apps for WordPress and Webflow are scheduled for Q2 2027.
